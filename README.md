## Project Overview

Job Market Intelligence is an end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes AI and data-related job postings to uncover hiring trends, required skills, and market demand.

Version 1 demonstrates the complete analytics workflow, from data collection and preprocessing to business intelligence and machine learning.

---

## Objectives

- Collect and clean AI & Data job postings.
- Standardize job titles and employment information.
- Extract technical skills from job descriptions using NLP.
- Analyze market demand across different job roles.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Train a machine learning model to predict job roles based on required skills.
